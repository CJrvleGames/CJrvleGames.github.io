CJrvle

A point-and-click game where you smash pots, collect coins, buy upgrades, and smash even more pots.

Download on Steam

Pots and hammers! Make progress by upgrading your pot and hammer through 26 different tiers.

Upgrades! Purchase upgrades in the shop with coins or rare diamonds to assist your pot smashing experience.

Zones! As you collect more coins, you will find yourself among 4 special zones. Discover the zones and defend against the enemies who reside in them.

Enemies! You are not alone, fight against 12 different enemies trying to steal your coins and claim their relics.

Relics! Discover 12 unique relics and equip up to 3 for helpful stat boosts and new mechanics.

Flowers! Care for 13 different types of flowers in your garden.
